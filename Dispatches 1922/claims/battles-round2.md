# Orders of Battle, round 2: sources

Four more battles: `chelyabinsk` (July 1919) and `irkutsk` (January and February 1920) for Siberia, `voronezh` (October and November 1919) and `perekop` (November 1920) for the Bolsheviks. Every weight, arm and enemy setup is a game judgment; each config says so in `modelled`. Two hosting orders were given a roll for this: `chelyabinskGrinder19` (the counterattack, whose documented result stays the likelier outcome at 75%) and `perekopAssault20` (the Sivash crossing, whose documented success stays the likelier outcome at 70%). `irkutskUltimatum20` and `cavalryArmyDebate19` already had rolls.
