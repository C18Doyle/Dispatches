# Preludes and "What this game leaves out": sources, first pass

Text: `PRELUDES` and `HARD_HISTORY` in `src/parts/30-screens.jsx`. These are not reports, so they are not in the claims register (`claims/*.json`); this table is their register. Status values are the same as in `claims/README.md`. Nothing here is `independent`: a reviewer has not seen it.

Method: web search on 2026-10-14, reading search summaries and Wikipedia pages, not the books. `search-summary` means the page itself was not opened. The wording of each sentence was chosen to say no more than the sources agree on.

**For the reviewer, first:** the sentences marked NEEDS A READER are the ones with the least support and the most weight. The page is about killing, and it names perpetrators on every side, so a wrong figure or a wrongly attributed act matters more here than in a report.
