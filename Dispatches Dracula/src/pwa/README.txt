Dracula: The Passage  (version __VERSION__)
A branching story of Bram Stoker's novel. Play Count Dracula, or switch to Mina Harker on the title page.

HOW TO PLAY
Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). That is the whole game: one file, no install, no network needed.
Your saves, settings and endings are kept in that browser on that computer. Clearing the browser's site data erases them.

PUTTING IT ON YOUR PHONE
Copy this folder to any web host that serves https (GitHub Pages, Netlify, your own server). Open the address on your phone and choose
"Add to Home Screen" (or "Install app"). The game then opens full screen and works offline. (A page opened straight from a file cannot be
installed; browsers only allow that over https.)

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html            the game
  manifest.webmanifest  the name, colours and icons for "Add to Home Screen"
  sw.js                 lets the installed copy work offline
  icon-192.png, icon-512.png
  README.txt            this file
