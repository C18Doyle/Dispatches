How this works. Each box below is one statement the game makes (a date, a figure, who did what, or how a person's view is put). Read it, look it up in your own source, and choose: Confirmed (type the source: a book and page, or a link), Wrong (say what is right), Needs a source (you could not find one), or Skip. Positions ("X's view is put as...") are characterizations, not quotations: confirm them if the wording is fair to the person. Counterfactual outcomes ("Speculative...") need no check. Your answers are kept in this browser as you go; Export my review saves a small file to send back. Nothing leaves your computer.
