# Ideas proven (or planned) in 1914 to bring back to 1940 and 1941

Written 2026-10-12 at the owner's request. Each item names the 1914 files that hold the working version.

## Content ideas the owner chose for 1914 and wants in 1940 and 1941 as well
1. **A second fork wave**: turn two-order decisions into three-way ones and add rolls. Measure with `Dispatches 1914/tools/balance.mjs` and read `docs/BALANCE_NOTES.md` first (detours are entered by 31 to 39 percent of files; do not gate the record's own order).
2. **A prelude per command**: the weeks before the war or the campaign opens, so the first real decision has a past.
3. **Deepen the thinnest command** (1914: Ottoman, 13 decisions a run; 1941/1940: check the same count with the balance tool).
4. **More battles**: Orders of Battle for actions that have no plan screen yet; one naval action, one set-piece from each side.
5. **Special events** (1914 `src/66-events.jsx`): short pieces triggered by a meter threshold or a flag, not by a node (a shell shortage, a mutiny, a strike, a newspaper campaign). 1940 already has wire bulletins and divergence headlines; events are the same idea driven by the state of the file.

## Flow and interface changes made in 1914 (port as they settle)
- **One calendar**: dates are shown as the UK writes them (4 August 1914) and always in the Western (Gregorian) calendar; Old Style (Russian) and Rumi (Ottoman) dates appear only in prose, once, as a note. The Settings screen says so.
- **Choose a command, then set up the file**: the command cards say plainly which nation and which seat; difficulty and "take control of battle planning" are chosen on the briefing screen after the command, not on the menu (1940 already does this on its War Room screen).
- **Briefing, then the war room**: a context screen per command (where things stand, your seat, the question), then the decisions.
- **How to play** page replaces "What this game leaves out" and Feedback on the menu; the leaves-out list is a section of it.
- **Settings as a screen**, not a folded list: sections, a three-stop Text size slider (Standard, Larger, Largest), colours, type, sound, signing, the calendar note.
- Verdict-first outcome screen, a decision line with a jump to the orders, the stepped Order of Battle, the share card, the playthrough bot (`tools/playthrough.mjs`).
